About

Who I Am

Hey there! I'm a Digital Designer with a passion for creating impactful visual experiences across web, social, and print. Over the past several years, I've worked across marketing, advertising, and digital platforms helping brands communicate clearly and creatively.

Currently, I work as a Graphic Designer at Integrated Real Estate Group, where I design marketing campaigns, digital ads, event promotions, and branded materials that support senior living communities and their teams. I collaborate closely with marketing and sales to bring ideas to life while maintaining strong brand consistency across channels.

Before this role, I worked on the T-Mobile and Metro by T-Mobile brands, creating web, social, email, and digital merchandising assets used across high-visibility platforms and campaigns.

Outside of my career in design, I'm also the founder of DZN Collective, a small collectibles business focused on Pokémon trading cards and pop-culture collectibles. Building this brand has allowed me to explore entrepreneurship, e-commerce, community building, and creative marketing from the ground up.

Recently becoming a new dad has added an exciting new chapter to my life and continues to motivate me to grow both personally and professionally. When I'm not designing, you'll probably find me collecting vinyl records, exploring car culture, creating content, or connecting with other creatives and collectors. Always open to connecting with other designers, marketers, and creative thinkers.
